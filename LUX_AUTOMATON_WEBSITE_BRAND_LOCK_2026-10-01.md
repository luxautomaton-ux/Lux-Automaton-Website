## 1. Brand hierarchy
- **Lux Automaton founders: Asa and Torrey.**
- Asa and Torrey are the only people presented as founders unless Asa explicitly changes this later.
- The currently approved Lux Automaton staff imagery supplied in the 2026-10-01 website thread is the canonical public-facing team/cast for company marketing pages.
- Do not swap approved staff imagery for unrelated stock people or newly generated substitutes without explicit approval.
- **Lux Automaton** is the parent company and creator of the Lux ecosystem.
- **Lux Agent** is the primary customer-facing AI platform.
- **Lux Agent Desktop** is the full home/office customer front door.
- **Lux Agent USB** is the optional portable/travel companion, not the primary brain and not a required physical product.
- Internal infrastructure such as Hermes/Codex may be shown as technology behind the ecosystem, but should not compete with the primary customer story.

### Platform capabilities shown inside the ecosystem, not all as top-level navigation

- Lux Command Center — dashboards, oversight, approvals, coordination
- Lux Flow — customer-facing workflow and automation experience
- Lux Agent Builder — create/configure governed agents
- Lux Verify / LAVA — evidence-backed verification and assurance
- Lux AI Toolkit Club — practical tools, templates, training, resources
