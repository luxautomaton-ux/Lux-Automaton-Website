# LUX LOGO FAMILY — OFFICIAL LOCK
Date: 2026-10-01
Status: CANONICAL / DO NOT REDESIGN

The logo sheets supplied by Asa on 2026-10-01 are the official Lux Automaton logo family.
They override older substitute, generic, or experimental Lux marks when the corresponding brand is shown.

## Global usage rules
- Use the approved LIGHT MODE lockup/icon on light or white surfaces.
- Use the approved DARK MODE lockup/icon on black, navy, charcoal, video, or dark-glass surfaces.
- Use the full wordmark/lockup for page heroes, section headers, product/service cards with sufficient room, press/media, and branded downloads.
- Use the approved icon-only treatment for app tiles, compact cards, navigation utilities, badges, favicons, and small UI surfaces.
- Never stretch, skew, recolor, redraw, regenerate, restyle, or substitute the logo.
- Never recreate the wordmark with live text when the official lockup is intended.
- Preserve aspect ratio and clear space.
- Do not place an unrelated Lux product logo on another product's page.
- The Lux Automaton master mark is the company mark for global navigation, footer, company/about/founder pages, and parent-company attribution.

## Website placement lock
- Global nav/header: Lux Automaton master logo.
- Global footer: Lux Automaton master logo.
- Products page: each product receives its own official logo/icon where the product is identified.
- Services/solutions pages: use the corresponding official service/product mark only when that service is actually represented.
- Lux Agent Desktop and Lux Agent USB sections must use their own marks, not a generic Lux Automaton or generic Lux Agent substitute.
- Lux Foundation page must use the Lux Foundation identity.
- Lux World surfaces must use Lux World identity.
- Lux AI Kids surfaces must use the Lux AI Kids identity.
- Lux Verify, Flow, Messages, Voice, Connect, WarmConnect, Agent Builder, Agent Training, Toolkit Club, Business Launch OS, Care OS, and LawCheck AI must use their corresponding official marks when surfaced.
- Company attribution can appear alongside a sub-brand, but the sub-brand mark remains primary on that sub-brand's own surface.

## Source protection
The 2026-10-01 supplied logo sheets are the visual source of truth. Existing production-ready logo files in Lux Vault may be reused only when they match these approved designs. If an older asset does not match, do not silently substitute it; replace it with the approved family asset before public use.
