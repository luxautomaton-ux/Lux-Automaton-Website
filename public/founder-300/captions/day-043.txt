3 MOVES FOR MULTI-AGENT CUSTOMER FLOW

When I think about multi-agent customer flow, I keep coming back to three moves: 1) define the real outcome, 2) make the handoffs visible, 3) decide what still requires human judgment. Handoffs matter more than the number of agents. Map the customer journey before assigning agents.
