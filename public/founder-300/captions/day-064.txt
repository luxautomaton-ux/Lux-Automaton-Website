3 MOVES FOR CUSTOMER SUPPORT

When I think about customer support, I keep coming back to three moves: 1) define the real outcome, 2) make the handoffs visible, 3) decide what still requires human judgment. Good automation makes escalation easier. Define the handoff before automating replies.
