3 MOVES FOR HUMAN JUDGMENT

When I think about human judgment, I keep coming back to three moves: 1) define the real outcome, 2) make the handoffs visible, 3) decide what still requires human judgment. Automation should create room for judgment, relationships and creativity. Automate the repeatable and protect the irreplaceable.
