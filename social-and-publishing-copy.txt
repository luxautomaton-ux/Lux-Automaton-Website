SOCIAL CAPTION — LONG
🚀 What if the most exciting AI career has not been named yet?

Some future roles may sound familiar: designer, teacher, developer, engineer. Others may be completely new—robot coach, prompt director, AI safety detective, synthetic media editor, or community automation guide.

Kids do not need to predict every job title. They can prepare by practicing four durable skills:

✨ Creativity
🛡️ Judgment
💬 Communication
🧩 Systems thinking

LUX AI Kids turns those skills into projects where children ask better questions, test ideas, explain their choices, check their work, and build things that help people.

What future career would your child invent?

SOCIAL CAPTION — PARENT/CAREGIVER
Your child does not need to guess the exact job titles of 2040.

They need chances to practice creativity, judgment, communication, and systems thinking today. Our newest LUX AI Kids guide includes a family-friendly Future Career Lab for inventing a role that solves a real problem—while deciding what AI can assist with and what people must still decide.

SOCIAL CAPTION — EDUCATOR
Career exploration does not have to begin with a list of existing jobs.

Invite students to identify a real problem, invent a future role, map the people and tools in the system, define AI’s appropriate role, and present a responsible prototype. The included LUX AI Kids activity sheet is designed for classrooms, clubs, workshops, and family learning.
