Safe AI learning starts with better questions—not fear. 💡

Before kids prompt, upload, generate, or share, help them pause and ask:
1. What am I making?
2. What should stay private?
3. Do I have permission?
4. How will I check it?
5. Who could this help—or affect?

Creativity, consent, and care belong in every AI project.
