LINKEDIN POST
Automation fails when it starts with software instead of work.

Before buying another app, draw one map:
• What happens when a lead arrives?
• What happens after a quote?
• What happens after payment?
• What happens when someone forgets to follow up?

Then mark the breaks:
• No clear owner
• Missing information
• Manual re-entry
• Delayed approval
• Invisible status

Now AI has a real job. It can draft a reply, classify a request, summarize a call, build the next checklist, or prepare an invoice for review.

Our new Lux Automaton editorial guide shows small business owners how to map one workflow, find one broken handoff, and automate the smallest reliable step first.

One workflow. One win. Then the next.
